Accessibility Statement

Last updated: June 2026

Our commitment

A La Carte Miami is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We continually improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards.

Conformance status

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. This website includes:

  • Semantic HTML structure and descriptive headings
  • Keyboard navigation support
  • Visible focus indicators on interactive elements
  • Alternative text for meaningful images
  • An accessibility toolbar (text size, contrast, link highlighting, reduced motion)
  • Skip to main content link

Accessibility toolbar

Use the accessibility button at the bottom-left of every page to adjust text size, enable high contrast, highlight links, or reduce motion. Settings are saved in your browser.

Known limitations

Some third-party content (such as embedded maps) may not fully meet accessibility standards. We are working to improve these integrations.

Feedback

If you encounter accessibility barriers on this site, please contact us:

  • Email: info@alacartemia.com
  • Contact form

We try to respond within 5 business days.
